# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Being respectful and inclusive.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment of any kind.
- Insults, trolling, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards.
